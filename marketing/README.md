## ✒️ Typography

- **Headings / logo**: Cormorant Garamond (or Cormorant), weight 500 upright + 400 italic. Free on Google Fonts.
- **Sans / small caps**: Outfit weight 300, letter-spacing 6–14. Free on Google Fonts.

If your printer or your marketing team's editor doesn't render Cormorant italic, install it once from https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Cormorant+Garamond — the SVGs will then render perfectly.

## 🖨️ Printer instructions

**Business card**
- Size 89×54mm final, 95×60mm with bleed. Trim on bleed line.
- Paper: 300–350gsm matte or velvet. Consider soft-touch lamination.
- **Gold accents printed with real gold foil** for the monogram, wordmark and corner marks — 10× more luxurious than 4-colour print.
- If foil not available: 4-colour CMYK on matte works too, but ensure gold hex `#c9a15a` prints slightly warmer (add 5% yellow).

**Brochure**
- 297×210mm A4 landscape, print both sides, Z-fold in thirds (fold at 99mm and 198mm from left).
- Paper: 200–250gsm matte. Silk finish also OK.
- The front cover monogram looks best with **spot UV or gold foil** on the "Art" letterforms.

**Letterhead**
- 210×297mm A4. 100–120gsm bright white or ivory.
- Print your body copy in `#4a4a4a` (soft grey), never pure black — matches the aesthetic.

## 📱 Instagram post usage

- Feed size is 1080×1080 (square) — post as-is.
- To use as a reel cover: crop centered vertical 1080×1350.
- Story: place inside a 1080×1920 story canvas with padding.

You can add product photos on top of the templates in Canva:
1. Open Canva → Upload the post PNG.
2. Add a "Photo" element behind or beside the type block.
3. Keep the gold ArtyFex ornaments intact — that's your visual signature.

## 🔄 Editing tips

- Open any `.svg` in Illustrator, Figma, Affinity Designer or Inkscape to edit text, swap colours, or drop photos in.
- Everything is vector — infinitely resizable without pixelation.
- If you want a variant (e.g. "Since 2019" instead of "Since 2020"), just find the string in the SVG file and change it — the rest of the design stays intact.
- PDFs are already print-ready — no additional work needed unless you want to change copy.
